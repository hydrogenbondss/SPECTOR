FAQ

Ray-Ban Meta teleprompter questions.

Can Gen 1 or Gen 2 glasses show a teleprompter?

No. Those models do not have a display. Spector gives their owners an adaptive rehearsal workflow on the phone so the final delivery can remain eyes-up.

I have Gen 1 / Gen 2 (no display). Can I still use Spector?

Yes. Rehearse on your phone with adaptive pacing and local feedback until the delivery feels natural, then perform without reading from a screen.

Is Spector made by Meta?

No. Spector is an independent, open-source teleprompter and is not affiliated with Meta or Ray-Ban. The product is designed to remain useful across multiple glasses platforms.

Is it really free?

Yes. The complete product available today is free, with no watermark or account requirement. Try it now.
