Pricing FAQ

Questions about paying for Spector.

Is the free tier actually free forever?

Yes. The core teleprompter — pacing, Comfort/Focus/Presentation modes, cue markers, analytics, PWA install — costs nothing and always will. Spector Pro is an optional upgrade for people who want the extra features listed above.

Is Spector Pro a subscription?

No — it's a one-time payment. There's no recurring charge, no auto-renewal, and no account required to use the free core.
