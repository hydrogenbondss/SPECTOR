FAQ

Three questions.

How is this different from voice-following teleprompters?

Those listen through a mic. Spector doesn't. Pace comes from your punctuation — same script, same run every time.

Is my script private?

Spector is free and open source. No account, no cloud, nothing uploaded — ever. Scripts stay in your browser.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Spector is free and open source (MIT) — the complete teleprompter, no account, watermark, or trial. The code is public on GitHub.
