Pricing FAQ

Questions about paying for Spector.

Is the free tier actually free forever?

Yes. The free product includes pacing, Comfort/Focus/Presentation modes, cue markers, analytics, and PWA installation. These features cost nothing and always will. And because Spector is open source and works offline, the free core can't be taken away — even if this project ended tomorrow, the app you have keeps working. Pro is optional.

Is Spector Pro a subscription?

No — Spector Pro is a one-time $34 purchase, never a subscription. You pay once and every Pro feature is yours as it ships. The core teleprompter stays free either way.

Do beta testers have to pay later?

No. Joining the hardware beta does not start a trial, deadline, subscription, or automatic paid conversion. Founding hardware testers who complete feedback get Spector Pro free for life.

Can I buy Pro today?

Yes — $34, one-time, right on this page or the home page. Founding hardware testers who join the beta get Pro free for life instead.

What's your refund policy?

Spector Pro comes with a 14-day no-questions refund — see the refund policy. Purchases run through Paddle, an independent Merchant of Record, so you can also request a refund directly through Paddle using the link in your order email — you never depend on us replying. The free core needs no refunds: there's nothing to pay.

Who do I contact about billing?

Email hello@spectorlabs.io. This is a solo-built project, so replies are best-effort, but every message gets read.
