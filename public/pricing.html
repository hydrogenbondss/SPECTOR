Pricing FAQ

Questions about paying for Spector.

Is the free tier actually free forever?

Yes. The free product includes pacing, Comfort/Focus/Presentation modes, cue markers, analytics, and PWA installation. These features cost nothing and always will. A future Pro tier would be optional.

Is Spector Pro a subscription?

No billing model has been finalized because Pro is not yet a product. Any future price, billing terms, and included features will be stated before checkout opens. The current teleprompter remains free.

Do beta testers have to pay later?

No. Joining the hardware beta does not start a trial, deadline, subscription, or automatic paid conversion. Hardware testers who complete feedback will receive a complimentary Pro license if Pro launches.

Can I buy Pro today?

Not yet. The free core is the product today. Pro checkout will open only after the paid features are ready and the final price is clearly posted.

What's your refund policy?

See the full refund policy. In short: reach out and we'll make it right.

Who do I contact about billing?

Email hello@spectorlabs.io. This is a solo-built project, so replies are best-effort, but every message gets read.
